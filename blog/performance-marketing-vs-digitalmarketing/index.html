A practical guide for businesses

Performance marketing vs digital marketing

Publishing a guide that brings people to your website and running an ad that brings in bookings are both digital marketing. The second takes a performance approach when you track which bookings the ad produced and what each one cost.

Digital marketing

How do customers discover your business and get to know it?

  • Search visibility through SEO and local listings
  • Articles, video, and social media
  • Email, paid ads, and your website

Performance marketing

Which campaign led someone to take a specific action?

  • A qualified call or WhatsApp enquiry
  • A purchase, booking, or registration
  • A subscription or app installation

How the two approaches compare

Performance marketing sits within digital marketing. The same channel can serve either purpose. What changes is the goal you set and the evidence you use to judge success.

Digital and performance marketing compared by goal, measures, and timeframe
AreaDigital marketingPerformance marketing
GoalBuild visibility, trust, and customer relationshipsGenerate and measure leads, bookings, or sales
MeasuresSearch rankings, reach, engagement, and trafficCost per lead, acquisition cost, and return on ad spend
TimingSEO and content can keep attracting visitors over timePaid campaigns can give earlier feedback on an offer

From a search to a customer

Imagine someone searches for “digital marketing consultant for small business.” Here’s how the channels can work together.

  1. Get discovered

    An SEO guide answers the searcher’s question. A Google Search ad can also reach them at the moment they’re looking for help.

  2. Earn the enquiry

    The visitor reads a case study, checks reviews, and opens the booking page. A follow-up email or a Meta remarketing ad on Facebook or Instagram can bring them back.

  3. Connect the enquiry to a sale

    Track the form submission or call, then check which leads became customers. A campaign that brings fewer enquiries can still produce more bookings.

What to measure

Pick the measure that answers your business question. If you sell consultations, the cost of a booked customer tells you more than the price of a click alone.

Cost per click (CPC)
Ad spend divided by clicks. What did you pay, on average, for someone to click your ad?
Cost per lead (CPL)
Ad spend divided by leads. What did you pay for each enquiry?
Cost per acquisition (CPA)
Campaign cost divided by acquisitions. What did it cost to gain a customer?
Conversion rate
The share of visitors who complete the action you’re measuring, such as booking a call.
Return on ad spend (ROAS)
Revenue attributed to ads divided by ad spend. How much revenue did each advertising rupee generate?

Choose a strategy for your goal

  • People aren’t finding you. Start with local SEO, useful content, and a website that explains your services clearly.
  • People visit but rarely enquire. Check the landing page and contact flow. Track form completions before paying for more traffic.
  • You need more qualified bookings. Test a focused Google or Meta campaign. Compare the cost of customers acquired across ads.

Use what you learn twice. Search terms that generate qualified leads can shape your next SEO article. A helpful article can also answer questions visitors still have after clicking an ad.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Picking a platform first. Define the audience and business outcome before choosing Instagram, search ads, or another channel.
  • Counting only clicks and followers. Track enquiries and sales so you can see where people drop out.
  • Relying on ads alone. The website, offer, content, and customer experience all affect whether someone buys.
  • Judging every channel on the same timeline. Give SEO and content time to build visibility while using paid campaigns for faster feedback.

Frequently asked questions

What is performance marketing vs digital marketing?
Digital marketing is the broader ecosystem of online marketing activities, including SEO, content, social media, email and advertising. Performance marketing is a results-focused approach within digital marketing that emphasizes measurable actions such as leads, sales and customer acquisitions.
Is performance marketing the same as digital marketing?
No. Performance marketing falls within the broader digital marketing ecosystem but focuses more heavily on measurable conversion and acquisition outcomes.
What is an example of performance marketing?
A Google Ads campaign designed to generate qualified enquiries and optimized according to cost per lead and customer acquisition data is an example of performance-focused marketing.
Do I need both digital marketing and performance marketing?
The appropriate combination depends on your business objectives. Digital marketing can support awareness and long-term visibility, while performance marketing can focus more directly on measurable customer acquisition.
