About Reshma Suresh

Digital Marketing Consultant in Kerala Helping Businesses Grow Online

I’m Reshma Suresh, a Digital Marketing Consultant and SEO Expert based in Kerala, with 8+ years of experience helping businesses build stronger online visibility, attract qualified leads, and achieve sustainable growth.

An MSc Mathematics and B.Ed graduate, my professional journey began as a full-time Freelance Digital Marketer (2018–2019), where I worked directly with businesses and gained hands-on experience in SEO, website optimization, content, social media, and digital advertising.

I later joined Codxel Technologies as an SEO Analyst (2020–2022), working extensively on keyword research, technical SEO, website audits, competitor analysis, content optimization, and organic search performance.

From 2022 to 2024, I worked as an SEO Manager at Nexxa Corporates Info Tech Pvt. Ltd., where my role expanded to include SEO strategy, project management, client communication, local search, website performance, and broader digital campaigns. During this period, I also worked as an SEO Trainer at Nexxa Digital Academy, sharing practical industry knowledge with aspiring digital marketers.

Since 2024, I have been working as an SEO Expert at Shaadow Digital Marketing Pvt. Ltd., focusing on search strategy, website optimization, analytics, paid advertising, and performance-driven digital marketing.

Throughout my career, I have contributed to 300+ global projects and completed 1,000+ SEO audits across different industries and markets. My experience now extends beyond traditional SEO to Local SEO, AEO, GEO, Google Ads, social media marketing, web design, web development, and AI-powered marketing.

Training has also become an important part of my professional journey. I have trained 750+ students through practical sessions, personal mentoring, and online classes. I also hold certifications from Google, HubSpot, Microsoft, and Semrush.
